# MediaPipe Pose Landmarker model

`pose_landmarker_lite.task` is the official MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite model.
It is distributed under the Apache License 2.0. The model source is the
[MediaPipe model collection](https://developers.google.com/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker#models).

The model is used for experimental 2D landmark estimation. Its output is not a
validated ergonomic or safe/unsafe lifting classification.
